# SAMKRUSHI — Business decisions index

**Last updated:** 2026-11-23

This file is the index of founder decisions. Approved decisions are recorded as ADRs in `docs/adr/` (ADR-0001). Open decisions are listed below, grouped by the master phase (master §10) that each one blocks.

**Rule:** agents must never invent prices, fees, taxes, supplier details, stock, cutoffs, slot windows, PIN codes, contact details or business policies. Each "Dev-only placeholder" applies **only to local/dev environments**. Placeholders are clearly synthetic, live in dev-only seeds or settings, and must not reach staging/production without founder sign-off.
